Field Note 06 · Hospitality · Greece · 2023–now · By Maor Harel

PLAY · BROWN · PAREA

A beautiful rooftop is an asset. A rooftop people deliberately return to is an operation.

The brief

The work across PLAY, BROWN and PAREA involved event production and content direction across more than twelve properties in Greece. The spaces were different — rooftops, pools, bars and terraces — but the operating question was consistent: how do you turn hospitality real estate into a recurring reason to visit?

The answer could not be a single event. It needed a calendar, talent, production discipline, guest flow, content and the ability to make the idea survive real service conditions.

The published record: three hotel brands, twelve-plus properties programmed in Greece, with event production and content direction across hospitality spaces.

What we actually owned

Programming sounds creative, but most of its value is operational. A strong booking at the wrong property is weak programming. A good event without guest flow creates frustration. A beautiful piece of content that overpromises the night damages the next visit. Every layer has to agree about what the room is trying to be.

That required thinking across brand, venue, audience and execution at the same time. The calendar had to create rhythm rather than random activity. Talent had to fit the space. VIP flow had to work without making the rest of the room feel secondary. The person on the floor at midnight mattered as much as the deck prepared weeks earlier.

What made it hard

Multi-property work adds complexity quickly. The temptation is to create one format and repeat it everywhere. That is efficient but often wrong. A pool, a rooftop and a hotel bar have different physical behaviour, different audiences and different reasons for existing. The system needs common standards without flattening the character of each property.

What stayed with us

The strongest hospitality programming does not feel added onto the venue. It makes the venue easier to understand. Guests know when to come, what kind of energy to expect and why that property belongs in their week. That is the difference between events as decoration and programming as an operating layer.
