Artists, designers, promoters, producers. People who actually live in the cities we work in and can tell you what changed last month.

They are not employees. They are not a roster we rent out. They are the reason a brief comes back with a real answer instead of a search result.

We don't know everything.
We know people who do.

Sometimes the best information is online. Sometimes it is sitting at the bar next to someone who has been running the room for ten years. The network is the asset — the work is how we use it.
