The company · Founder-led

BUILT BY AN operator.

Dubel Team is a founder-led operator company. Not an agency, not a personal portfolio — a company that takes ownership of the brief and runs it to a number.

For fifteen-plus years the work has been the same shape: take on a hard operational problem, own the P&L, and deliver in the real world rather than in a slide deck. That's happened across six industries — hospitality, cultural production, sports, strategy, events and market entry — and in each one the same discipline applied. Operators first. Advisors second.

The company was founded by Maor Harel and operates from Athens, where it has run hospitality and cultural programming across Greece since 2021. With EU citizenship and a clean local footprint, Dubel Team is the on-the-ground partner for brands and operators who need Greece to work — and the strategic operator for anyone who needs a brief turned into execution, anywhere.

Every project is built from scratch to fit the brief, the client and the room. Nothing off the shelf, nothing handed to a junior team. The result is the only proof that matters: the receipts.
