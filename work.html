SPORT & fan culture.

It started on a terrace, not in an office. A club owned by the people who go to the games — built from the lowest division upward, with the membership, the culture and the commercial engine assembled by hand.

01 2007 — 2015

HAPOEL TEL AVIV B.C.

Founder · Israel's first fan-owned professional club
Basketball · Tel Aviv

A professional basketball club built from nothing and owned by its supporters — the first of its kind in Israel. Eight years from the lowest division to the Premier League, and every layer underneath it built by hand: the membership model, the fan culture, the merchandise, the matchday operation and the commercial engine that paid for all of it. A 3,500-seat arena was planned around the club it became.

Founder · Pop-up street-art museum
Athens · Greece

A street-art museum founded from zero in an Athens courtyard: the venue, the curation, the fourteen artists, the €100K budget, the permits, the bar, the programme and the people on the door. Ten thousand visitors came through a space that did not exist six months earlier — the clearest single proof of what "operator, not advisor" actually means.

The honest version

WHO'S THE team?

Fair question. There is no floor of desks behind this.

Dubel Team is run by one operator. What stands behind it is a network — people who know their world from the inside. Hospitality operators and chefs. Bartenders and DJs. Artists, designers, promoters, producers. People who actually live in the cities we work in and can tell you what changed last month.

They are not employees. They are not a roster we rent out. They are the reason a brief comes back with a real answer instead of a search result.

We don't know everything.
We know people who do.

Sometimes the best information is online. Sometimes it is sitting at the bar next to someone who has been running the room for ten years. The network is the asset — the work is how we use it.
