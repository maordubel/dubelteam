The last video had gone up two months earlier. Mehran, in front of a whiteboard, solving an equation with two unknowns. At the end of the video he smiled at the camera and said, in English: &quot;Two unknowns. One solution. Don't give up on it.&quot;</p><p data-i="30">She wrote a comment. Deleted it. Wrote it again.
