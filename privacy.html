PRIVACY & cookies.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Dubel Team respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use dubelteam.com, how it may be used, and how advertising and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Information you provide

If you contact us through the site or by email, we may receive information such as your name, email address, company or project name, and the content of your message. We use that information only to respond to your enquiry, discuss potential work, maintain business records, and protect the site from abuse.

Contact form provider

The contact form is processed through a third-party form delivery service. Information submitted through the form may therefore be transmitted to and processed by that service in order to deliver your message to Dubel Team. If you prefer, you can contact us directly by email instead.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site may use Google AdSense and other Google advertising services. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to serve, measure and personalise advertising, subject to your consent where required by law.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user's visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner sites information page.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Cookies and consent

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for essential site operation, security, analytics, advertising and measurement. Where required, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be presented with a consent message before consent-dependent advertising technologies are used.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site or advertising function.

Server and technical data

Like most websites, hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and security logs. This information is used for site delivery, reliability, diagnostics and abuse prevention.

How information is used

  • To operate, secure and maintain the website.
  • To respond to enquiries and communicate about requested services.
  • To understand site performance and improve the user experience.
  • To display and measure advertising where enabled.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, deliver contact-form messages, host infrastructure, provide analytics or serve advertising. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the site, its users or our rights.

Retention

Enquiry information and related business correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations or protect legitimate interests. Technical logs are retained according to the practices of the relevant hosting and infrastructure providers.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Other Dubel Team products

Dubel Team operates separate digital products on subdomains and related properties. Those products may have additional privacy notices or product-specific data practices. Where a product has its own privacy notice, that notice applies to use of that product.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact maordubel@gmail.com.
