THE Worker

Football history you can enter.

The Worker is an interactive Hapoel Tel Aviv universe that combines a narrative supporter game with a growing historical archive. The player moves through real eras, places, supporter culture and major club moments rather than reading a static chronology.

Its content expands through research, new historical periods, archive material and interactive gates covering trivia, shirts, line-ups, supporter memories and club history. The product is designed for repeat exploration as more of the historical world is opened.

DUbid

A fantasy game that lives with every round.

Dubid is a Hebrew-first fantasy football game built around the Israeli league. Players build teams in 5×5 and 11×11 modes, make captain and squad decisions, and compete using the outcomes of real league matches.

The product changes with the season: fixtures, squads, results, scoring, standings and gameweek decisions all create a reason to return. It is maintained as a live competition rather than published once as a football statistics page.

OFFsides

Football gives you the moment. You make the call.

Offsides turns football moments, predictions and judgments into quick decisions. Players make calls, compare outcomes, build scores and compete in recurring rounds and arenas rather than passively consuming another football feed.

New football creates new questions and new competition. Match context, rounds, arenas, scoring and standings keep the product moving, while the game systems, sharing and realtime features are maintained as an active service.

FAN Life

One engine. Many clubs.

Fan Life takes what The Worker proved for one club and turns it into a platform: each club is a data pack with its own identity, squads, seasons, kits and supporter culture, played through shared timelines, gates and LIFE experiences.

Research comes first. New clubs are built from reviewed, sourced evidence, and where the data is thin the game says so with shorter, labelled rounds rather than inventing history. Today the shared Timeline plays for Hapoel Tel Aviv, HŠK Zrinjski Mostar and Olympiacos.

GOOD TO know.

Who runs them?

Dubel Team, a founder-led company in Athens. Every game here is built and operated in-house; see our product standards for how they are maintained and corrected.

Why are some games in Hebrew?

Dubid and The Worker serve Israeli supporters first and are Hebrew-first. Offsides and Fan Life are designed to travel across languages and clubs.

Something is wrong or missing — who do I tell?

Use the contact page. Corrections are reviewed against sources and fixed in the product.

Why the games matter

A product has value when somebody has a reason to come back. Fresh rounds, new decisions, expanding history, scoring and competition are not SEO copy — they are the operating loops inside these products. We build the software, maintain the systems and keep the content moving.
